Privacy policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026
App: MergeFlare · Developer: Orbita Labs
Contact: orbitalabs@outlook.com

Game data on your device

MergeFlare stores your best score, tutorial completion, language, sound preferences and an advertising-session flag locally on your device. Orbita Labs does not receive this game data. No player account or online leaderboard is required.

Advertising and privacy choices

Versions with advertising use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). These services may process IP addresses (which can be used to estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics and performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and comply with applicable requirements. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on availability, region and privacy choices. Non-personalized ads may still process data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Where required, the app presents Google's consent form before requesting ads. You can revisit your choices through Menu → Privacy choices when this option is provided for your region. You can use the core game without watching the optional BACK rewarded ad. Interstitial ads may appear between games. Test builds use Google test ad units; this does not make them a guarantee of no network data processing.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Orbita Labs does not receive your advertising identifier or IP address directly through its own backend.

No separate analytics or account backend

The current app does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or an Orbita Labs account backend. AdMob's own advertising measurement and diagnostics are covered above. Fonts and game audio are bundled with the app.

Sharing and support

Sharing is optional. The app opens your device's share sheet with an image and text of your game result. You choose the recipient and service; Orbita Labs does not receive a copy. If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use it to respond to your request.

Retention and deletion

You can clear local game data using your device's app-storage controls. Uninstalling removes the app's local data; your device's backup settings may retain or restore it. Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the contact address above. Google manages retention of advertising data under its own policies and controls.

Children

The app does not ask players to submit personal details. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Orbita Labs through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Ad and audience settings for each release must match its store declarations.

Security, international processing and rights

We limit the information requested for support. Service providers such as Google and the email provider may process information outside your country under their applicable safeguards. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to processing or withdraw consent. Contact us for information handled by Orbita Labs; use Google's controls for data it handles independently. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Updates

We will update this page when our data practices change and show the effective date above.
