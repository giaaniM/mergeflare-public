Privacy policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026
App: MergeFlare · Developer: Orbita Labs
Contact: orbitalabs@outlook.com

Game data on your device

MergeFlare stores your best score, tutorial progress, game-session counters, language and sound preferences locally on your device. Orbita Labs does not receive this game data. No player account or online leaderboard is required.

No advertising or analytics SDKs

The current release of MergeFlare does not display ads and does not include Google AdMob, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or another analytics SDK. Orbita Labs does not collect gameplay telemetry through the app. Fonts and game audio are bundled with the app.

Google Play may independently provide store statistics and technical diagnostics under its own settings and privacy policy. See Google's Privacy Policy.

No player account

The game does not require registration and has no Orbita Labs account backend or online leaderboard.

Sharing and support

Sharing is optional. The app opens your device's share sheet with an image and text of your game result. You choose the recipient and service; Orbita Labs does not receive a copy. If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use it to respond to your request.

Retention and deletion

You can clear local game data using your device's app-storage controls. Uninstalling removes the app's local data; your device's backup settings may retain or restore it. Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the contact address above.

Children

The app does not ask players to submit personal details. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Orbita Labs through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Security, international processing and rights

We limit the information requested for support. Service providers such as Google and the email provider may process information outside your country under their applicable safeguards. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to processing or withdraw consent. Contact us for information handled by Orbita Labs; use Google's controls for data it handles independently. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Updates

We will update this page before introducing advertising or other services that change how information is handled, and show the effective date above.
